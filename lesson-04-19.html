Урок 19 (Модуль 4) — 🍷 Воскресный обед с тестем (casual, 23.08)

Сцена: обед у родителей жены. Григорий, 72, бывший инженер, репатриант из Москвы, 30 лет в Израиле, год жил в Штатах — английский хороший. Сегодня в настроении поспорить.

Задача урока: удержать семейный регистр в споре. Не переходить в лекцию, не защищаться профессионально. Это тесть, а не Dr. Patel.

🎬 Полный диалог — как он прошёл

Grigory: So, Eduard. I was reading about artificial intelligence in medicine. They say in ten years, computers will do your job better than you. Diagnose faster, never get tired, never make mistakes. What do you think? Should my grandson go to medical school, or is it a dying profession?

Ты: Yeah, Grigory, this is one of the interesting questions these days. Sometimes this issue comes up in our discussions at work. To be honest, this is my main concern about the future of medicine. You are absolutely right about the diagnosing, tiring and mistakes. If you ask me, it'll take at least 5-10 years. I don't think that medicine is going to die, and I still believe that my son should go to medical school.

Grigory: (смеётся) Not radiology — okay, I'll tell him. (серьёзнее) But let me ask you something else. My daughter says you work too much. Sixty hours, seventy sometimes. When I was your age I worked like that too, and you know what? I don't remember my kids being small. I remember the projects. (смотрит прямо) Is it worth it, Eduard?

Ты: I know my wife is worried about it. And you know what, I couldn't agree more. It's not as simple as it seems. Easier said than done. You're right about kids. I'm missing out on a lot. It's not worth it, Grigory. On the other hand, if you want to succeed at what you do, you have to work hard: extra hours, extra duties. Nothing in life is free.

Grigory: She didn't complain. She's proud of you, actually — she just worries. (после паузы) You know what I did wrong? I thought there would be time later. There's always later. And then one day my son is thirty-five and lives in Berlin and we talk twice a year. (поднимает бокал) Anyway. Enough philosophy at lunch. To your health — and to your kids growing up with a father around.

Ты: Thanks for telling me. I will talk to her. I am sorry to hear about your son. I won't let that happen with my kids. Thank you. L'chaim.

✍️ Тот же разговор — как сказал бы носитель

Про ИИ и внука:
Ha — funny you ask, we talk about this at work all the time.
And honestly? You're not wrong. They'll diagnose faster than me, they won't get tired, they won't miss things at four in the morning. That part's true.
But here's the thing — that's maybe twenty percent of what I do. The rest is sitting with a family telling them their father isn't waking up. Show me the machine that does that.
So yeah — send him to medical school. Just not to radiology 😄

На «is it worth it»:
Yeah. That's the part that scares me.
And she's right — I'm missing a lot of it. Football games, dinners, bedtime. I know.
Honestly? Some days I don't think it's worth it. But I don't know another way to do this job — if I want to be good at it, something's got to give.
She said that to you? ...That's not nothing, Grigory. I'll talk to her.

Финал:
Thanks for telling me. I'll talk to her.
And Grigory — twice a year, that's rough. I'm sorry.
I'll try not to make the same mistake.
I'll drink to that. To you too. L'chaim.

💊 Косвенная речь (грамматика т.13) — в бою

Правило: прошлое снаружи → прошлое внутри.

Прямая речьКосвенная
"I am tired."He said he was tired
"We started antibiotics."They said they had started antibiotics
"I will call you."He said he would call me
"I can do it."He said he could do it

Механика: present → past · past → had + V3 · will → would · can → could · may → might

Не сдвигаются: would, could, should, might, had — уже сдвинуты.

⚠️ Вопрос в косвенной речи теряет вопросительный вид

❌ <s>She asked, "Did I check the lactate?"</s>
✅ She asked if I had checked the lactate.

Механика: убрать кавычки → добавить if/whether → подлежащее вперёд → сдвинуть время. Знака вопроса в конце нет.

⚠️ say vs tell

tell someone something (адресат обязателен) · say something (to someone)

✅ He told me he was leaving · ❌ <s>He said me</s>

Указатели времени

today → that day · yesterday → the day before · tomorrow → the next day · now → then · here → there

✅ Когда сдвиг не нужен

Если факт всё ещё верен: He said the ICU is full · The nurse said the patient is refusing the tube ✅
